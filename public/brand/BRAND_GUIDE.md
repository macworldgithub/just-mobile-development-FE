# JustMobile active brand guide

Prepared by OmniSuiteAI Pty Ltd. Working identity baseline beced0d6, 28 October 2026. Brand and trademark approval required before production.

## Identity

Use the lowercase `justmobile.` wordmark with the four-part rounded mark. The headline is “Less telco. More you.” Supporting copy is “A little less telco. A lot more you.” Do not substitute the originally supplied legacy logo; it is retained in the reference folder for migration context only.

Use the ink wordmark on light surfaces and the light wordmark on ink surfaces. The outlined SVG files require no font installation. Raster files have transparent backgrounds. The mark variants are intended for the favicon, small identity placements and app/social contexts. App-platform packaging and final safe-area checks are still required if a native app is later commissioned.

As a proposed minimum, leave at least half a mark-width of clear space around the full wordmark and one-quarter mark-width around an isolated mark. Do not distort, rotate the whole logo beyond its existing mark geometry, apply decorative gradients or recolour the wordmark arbitrarily. These spacing rules are new production recommendations and should be ratified with brand approval.

## Colour and typography

Ink #142420 anchors headings, navigation and strong actions. Lime #D5F879 is the distinctive highlight and must be paired with dark text rather than white text. Paper #FAFBF8 gives the experience a warm, restrained backdrop. Supporting colours, typography weights, breakpoints and spacing are specified in `design-tokens.json`.

Manrope is used for headings and the wordmark; Inter is used for body text and UI. Fonts and their original licences are bundled in `fonts/`. Normal body content and legal/financial disclosures must remain readable on phones. The current design already permits zoom and reduced motion; the complete production interface still requires accessibility verification.

## Voice and AI presentation

Speak as a professional mobile brand: clear, calm and useful. Lead with the service and the complete price. Explain “pending” and “action required” without pretending the network has confirmed something it has not. Distinguish handset payments from mobile-service payments and show the agreed final-payment adjustment.

Do not make AI the main buying proposition. Bele should be a discreet help mechanism, not a dramatic hero feature or a substitute for structured checkout and activation. Be honest that assistance is automated when relevant; do not impersonate a human adviser. Routine automation belongs in reliable operations, not promotional claims about having no staff.

Avoid unsupported cheapest/first/instant claims, invented reviews, retail-network equivalence and any “never fails” promise. Approved network, finance and service qualifiers must be visible where they affect the offer.

## Imagery and provenance

Use only the active files in `images/` unless a new asset is approved. Reconfirm that handset imagery corresponds to the exact sellable market variant, storage and colour. Supplier image use and photograph rights remain approval items. The asset manifest includes provenance notes and file hashes. The pack does not transfer ownership of third-party images or fonts.
